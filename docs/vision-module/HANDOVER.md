# Vision Module handover (2026-10-22, version v0)

1. What I built: `@sentinel/vision-detector` tracks a selected face and measures normalized mouth aperture, head pose, landmark stability, and hand overlap. `@sentinel/sync-detector` evaluates 8-second audio and mouth-motion windows. `@sentinel/challenge-verifier` checks head turns, face covering, and hand-near-face actions.
2. Pinned versions: `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` 1.1.1; face and hand task assets are the float16 v1 model bundles listed in `MODEL_CARD.md`.
3. Current validation: `pnpm verify` passes integration, privacy, typecheck, all 48 tests across 12 files, and the extension build. `pnpm build:harness` passes, and the browser self-test passes 20/20 checks with real modules; see `docs/validation-report.md` for limits.
4. Self-test fixture: `packages/vision-detector/assets/selftest/face.jpg` is a synthetic fictional portrait. It is for smoke testing face landmarks and frame-classifier inference only, not for accuracy claims. Real camera validation still requires a consenting participant.
5. Recommended settings: sync starts at `speechRms=0.01`, `rLow=0.1`, `rHigh=0.5`, and an 8-second window with ±400 ms lag search. These are defaults only; no labelled clips were available to calibrate them. Challenge artifact thresholds should not be selected until real and fake sessions are measured.
6. Inputs required from the team: the evaluation label file is header-only because no labeled real/fake clips were supplied. For accuracy claims, add authorized real/fake clips, calibrate sync thresholds, and measure challenge pass and artifact rates; do not fill these results with assumed values.
7. Known limits and failure cases: lip-sync sees mouth motion at roughly word and phrase level at 10 fps and cannot reliably detect short closures such as /p/. Sync is suitable for 1-on-1 calls only; group-call audio may not belong to the tracked face. MediaPipe face and hand overlap heuristics are experimental. The matrix/geometry pose sign convention and latency on 480 px live frames still need video validation.
8. Verification status: see `docs/validation-report.md` for current automated and browser smoke-test results. A synthetic fixture does not validate behavior on real callers. Accuracy on labeled clips, pose signs against a real call, real/fake sync distributions, challenge pass and artifact rates, and performance on a live Meet call remain unmeasured.
9. Scaffold additions needed: the installed MediaPipe Web bundle includes a sender for `https://odml.pa.googleapis.com/v1/log`; the official MediaPipe privacy notice says the SDK sends performance and utilization metrics, while keeping input images/video on-device. The project's source privacy scan does not inspect dependency code. Full-Stack Integration should set `connect-src 'self'` for the harness and extension pages, then verify in Chrome's Network panel that blocking this endpoint does not stop inference and that no external request succeeds. Do not claim zero egress before that check. [MediaPipe privacy notice](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/tasks).
10. Notes for the root README: describe the vision signals and challenge checks as a prototype. Do not claim accuracy, false-positive rates, calibrated thresholds, or validated deepfake detection until `eval/vision/report.md` contains measured results.

Resolution limit: with 100 ms audio bins and about 200 ms video frames, the sync check only resolves broad mouth opening and closing. It is not phoneme-level lip-sync detection. The captured tab audio is mixed in group calls; the sync result should be used for 1-on-1 calls. Surface the message “Works best in 1-on-1 calls” in the overlay.

## Frame-classifier training/evaluation update (2026-10-22)

- No new vision checkpoint was trained or exported. The workspace contains no labeled real/fake video or frame dataset; the vision label CSV is header-only. The synthetic `assets/selftest/face.jpg` remains smoke-test-only.
- The shipped CommunityForensics image model remains unchanged. Its source revision, asset hash, crop, resize, RGB normalization, tensor layout, and sigmoid output mapping are recorded in `MODEL_CARD.md` and regression-tested in `packages/vision-detector/tests/deepfake.test.ts`.
- No frame-level or video-level metrics, threshold, compression robustness, or whole-video detection claim is available. The browser emits a per-frame synthetic-image signal with fixed confidence 0.4; the downstream reason cutoff is not calibrated.
- For future labeled exports, use `python eval/vision/evaluate_frame_scores.py PATH_TO_SCORES.csv --dataset NAME --revision REVISION --license LICENSE`. The script requires train/validation/test labels and disjoint video, person, and source IDs; chooses a threshold on validation video scores only; freezes max-score-over-frames aggregation before test evaluation; and reports both frame and video metrics. It is a score-reporting CLI, not an ONNX inference harness.
- No video dataset was downloaded or added. The source image model's MIT code/model license does not supply a provenance audit for all of its underlying training images; no attempt was made to retrain it on unverified material.
