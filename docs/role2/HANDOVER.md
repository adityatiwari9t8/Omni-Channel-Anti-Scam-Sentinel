# Role 2 handover (2026-10-22, version v0)

What I built: `@sentinel/vision-detector` tracks a selected face and measures normalized mouth aperture, head pose, landmark stability, and hand overlap. `@sentinel/sync-detector` evaluates 8-second audio and mouth-motion windows. `@sentinel/challenge-verifier` checks head turns, face covering, and hand-near-face actions.

Sync is suitable for 1-on-1 calls only; group-call audio may not belong to the tracked face. MediaPipe face and hand overlap heuristics are experimental. The matrix/geometry pose sign convention and latency on 480 px live frames still need video validation.
